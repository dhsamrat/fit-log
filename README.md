# 🏋️ FitLog — Workout Library & Planner

**FitLog** is a modern and responsive workout management application designed to help users discover, organize, and plan their daily workouts in a simple and convenient way.

Users can explore a variety of workouts, view detailed exercise information, add workouts to **Today's Plan**, and save their favorite workouts for later. The application also provides a dedicated **My Plan** section where users can manage their selected workouts, track total workout time and calories, and sort exercises based on duration, calories, or rating.

## ✨ Features

### 1. 🏋️ Workout Library

Browse a collection of workouts with important information such as muscle groups, equipment, duration, calories burned, difficulty level, and ratings.

### 2. 📋 Today's Plan

Add workouts to your daily workout plan. The navbar automatically displays the current number of planned workouts.

### 3. ❤️ Save for Later

Save workouts that you are interested in and access them later from the **Saved** section of the My Plan page.

### 4. 📊 My Plan & Sorting

Manage all your selected workouts from one place. The My Plan section displays total workout duration and calories, with sorting options based on:

* Duration
* Calories
* Rating

### 5. 🔎 Workout Details

View complete information about any workout, including:

* Workout description
* Muscle groups
* Equipment
* Difficulty
* Duration
* Calories burned
* Sets & reps
* Rating
* Step-by-step instructions

Users can also add a workout directly to **Today's Plan** or save it for later.

## 📱 Responsive Design

FitLog is fully responsive and optimized for **desktop, tablet, and mobile devices**. The navigation, workout cards, grid layouts, plan section, and workout details automatically adjust to different screen sizes.

## 🎯 Project Goal

The main goal of FitLog is to provide a clean and user-friendly workout management experience where users can easily discover exercises, organize their daily routines, and keep track of their workout plans.
